Before the demo

Three questions first.

Every coaching business leaks money in a different place. These three answers decide what's worth building.

1

When someone buys your low-ticket offer or comments on a post, what happens in the first hour?

Why it matters: the first hour is when interest is highest. Who replies, how fast, and on which channel decides how many ever book a call.

2

Of the people who buy the entry offer or join the community, how many end up on a strategy call, and where do most drop off?

Why it matters: the money sits between the small offer and the big one. Knowing the drop-off point shows exactly where a system pays for itself.

3

If you could hand one part of your week to a system tomorrow, what would it be?

Why it matters: start where it gives back the most time or money, then build out from there.

How it stays legal (UK)
  • We only call or text people who ticked a separate consent box for it.
  • Every text names the business and says how to stop. Every call says it's an assistant.
  • No contact before 08:00 or after 20:00 in the lead's time zone.
  • A person can take over any conversation at any moment.

Funding and fees

Three ways to pay for your training

Fees depend on the course and its practicals. Ask us for the fee of your course and the payment options that fit you.

Sponsored

A company or foundation pays for a group of learners, often from its own community or staff.

  • No course fee for you
  • Your sponsor gets progress reports

SETA-funded

CATHSSETA and other SETA programmes fund learnerships. Many include a stipend for transport and costs.

  • A stipend may apply
  • We handle the registration

Self-funded

Pay for yourself in monthly instalments, so you do not need the full amount up front.

  • Pay in instalments
  • Fees on application

Questions

Good to know

Is there a housekeeping course near me?
Our housekeeping course runs as an online cohort, so you can join from home wherever you live in South Africa. The next group starts on 1 November.
Can I study online from anywhere?
Yes, for courses we run as online cohorts. Lessons and weekly sessions are online, and you show your practical skills by video. Courses like chef training need a working kitchen for the practical part.
How much does a course cost?
Fees depend on the course and its practicals, so fees are on application. Many learners are sponsored or SETA-funded, and self-funded learners can pay in instalments.
Do I need matric?
Each course has its own entry requirements. Many start with basic reading and writing and the motivation to learn. [entry requirements per course to confirm]
Is the certificate recognised?
Our courses are accredited, and we register learners with CATHSSETA. Your results are assessed and moderated by registered assessors before your certificate is issued.
What happens to my ID?
We use it only to register you with CATHSSETA and, if a sponsor pays for your training, to report to them. It is stored securely under POPIA and never sold. See how we protect it.

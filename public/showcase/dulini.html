A weekend in the Dulini inbox

One enquiry. Two weekends.

The same family, the same Saturday night. Switch between how it runs today and how it runs with Verdance. Example

Every step answered or handed over. Nothing waits for Monday.

  1. Saturday 20:40

    A family of five asks about the Sabi Sand in April

    It lands in the reservations inbox. The office closed at 16h00 on Friday. The next person to read it arrives on Monday at 08h00.
    It is read straight away. Two adults, three children aged 9, 12 and 15, four nights in April, Sabi Sand. Logged with consent, ready for a reply.
  2. Saturday 20:40, within a minute

    Answered in Dulini's voice

    No reply. The family keeps browsing, and sends the same message to other lodges in the reserve.
    A warm, personal reply goes out. It greets them the way Dulini would, confirms children over eight are welcome, and asks the one question that matters next: their dates.
  3. Saturday 20:42

    The right lodge suggested for the group

    Nobody is there to match them. They are left to compare five lodges on their own, on a phone, late on a Saturday.
    Matched to how they travel. Two adjacent suites so the children are close by, at the lodge that suits a family.
    Dulini RiverAlternative: Dulini Moya
  4. Saturday 20:43 to Sunday

    Dates checked, questions handled or handed over

    Their questions pile up. Flights, transfers, bush walks with children. By Sunday evening, they may have heard back from two other lodges.
    Dates checked against availability. Getting there is answered from your own information: Federal Air from Johannesburg into Ulusaba Airstrip, then your 4x4 transfer. Rates and the bush walk question go to a person, with the full conversation attached.
  5. Monday 08:00

    The reservations team opens the inbox

    A weekend backlog, replied to cold. The family's message is now 35 hours old, and it sits among everything else that came in since Friday.
    A short list, already replied to and flagged. The warm ones are on top, each with what is needed next. Your team starts Monday by confirming, not by catching up.

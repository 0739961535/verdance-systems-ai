How this is worked out
  1. Extra share = the within-a-minute share minus today's share.
  2. Extra bookings a week = after-hours enquiries a week x the extra share.
  3. A month = a week x 52 / 12. A year = a week x 52.
  4. Extra revenue = extra bookings x the average booking value, rounded to the nearest R1,000.

The cap: the within-a-minute share is never counted above double today's share, or 15 points above it, whichever is lower. Enquiries in office hours, repeat stays and referrals are left out, so the figure stays on the careful side. None of these numbers are Dulini's.
